🎉 Winston-Salem Family Adventure

3-Day Itinerary for Kids Ages 4 & 7

Day 1

Discovery & History

9:00 AM - 12:00 PM

🎨 Kaleideum Downtown

▼

Start your adventure at this amazing children's museum with interactive exhibits perfect for young explorers!

📍 390 S Liberty St, Winston-Salem, NC 27101
🎯 Highlights: The Edible Garden, Make It Studio, and Far Out! planetarium show
💡 Kids can build, create, and explore hands-on science exhibits at their own pace
💡 Parent Tip:
Arrive right when it opens to avoid crowds. Don't miss the sensory-friendly exhibits on the second floor!
12:30 PM - 1:30 PM

🍕 Lunch at Mellow Mushroom

▼

Fun, family-friendly pizza restaurant with a creative atmosphere kids will love!

📍 345 S Liberty St, Winston-Salem (walking distance!)
🍴 Kid favorites: Cheese pizza, pretzel bites, and fun drink options
💡 Parent Tip:
They have coloring sheets to keep kids entertained while waiting for food.
2:00 PM - 4:30 PM

🏛️ Historic Old Salem

▼

Step back in time and explore this living history museum with costumed interpreters and historic buildings.

📍 900 Old Salem Rd, Winston-Salem, NC 27101
🎯 Must-see: Winkler Bakery (fresh cookies!), T. Bagge Merchant, and toy museum
🎪 Kids can try on period clothing, play old-fashioned games, and see craftspeople at work
💡 Parent Tip:
Buy Moravian cookies at the bakery - they're amazing! The toy museum is especially engaging for the 7-year-old.
5:00 PM - 6:30 PM

🍔 Dinner & Ice Cream

▼

Casual dinner followed by sweet treats at local favorite ice cream shop.

🍴 Dinner option: Sweet Potatoes (Southern comfort food with kids menu)
🍦 Dessert: Walk to Footnotes Handcrafted Ice Cream nearby

Day 2

Outdoor Adventure & Play

9:00 AM - 12:30 PM

🌳 Tanglewood Park

▼

Massive park with playgrounds, trails, and tons of space for kids to run and explore!

📍 4061 Clemmons Rd, Clemmons, NC 27012 (15 min drive)
🎯 Activities: Playground, pedal boats (seasonal), easy walking trails, deer park
🦌 Visit the petting farm where kids can see goats, chickens, and other friendly animals
💡 Parent Tip:
Bring a picnic blanket and snacks. The playground is top-notch with equipment for both age groups. Pack bug spray in warmer months!
12:30 PM - 1:30 PM

🌮 Lunch at Village Tavern

▼

Family-friendly restaurant with diverse menu options for picky and adventurous eaters alike.

🍴 Great kids menu including chicken tenders, mac & cheese, and sliders
2:30 PM - 5:00 PM

🎭 Kaleideum North

▼

Environmental park and museum with nature trails, live animals, and outdoor exhibits.

📍 400 W Hanes Mill Rd, Winston-Salem, NC 27105
🎯 Highlights: Planetarium shows, live animal exhibits, Explore Outdoors trail
🐍 See snakes, turtles, owls, and other native North Carolina animals up close
💡 Parent Tip:
Check planetarium show times in advance. The outdoor trails are stroller-friendly and perfect for burning off energy!
6:00 PM - 7:00 PM

🍝 Dinner at Crafted

▼

Casual American dining with a welcoming atmosphere for families.

🍴 Popular items: Burgers, pizzas, and pasta that kids love

Day 3

Science & Nature Finale

9:30 AM - 12:30 PM

🔬 SciWorks

▼

Hands-on science center and environmental park - perfect finale to your Winston-Salem adventure!

📍 400 W Hanes Mill Rd, Winston-Salem, NC 27105
🎯 Must-do: BioWorks (touch sea stars!), physics exhibits, outdoor nature trails
🧪 Interactive exhibits about sound, light, health, and animals that kids can touch and explore
💡 Parent Tip:
The 4-year-old will love the water play area (bring a change of clothes!). Check for live science demonstration times.
1:00 PM - 2:00 PM

🥪 Lunch at Panera Bread

▼

Quick, healthy lunch option with kid-friendly choices.

🍴 Kids menu: Mac & cheese, grilled cheese, PB&J, and more
2:30 PM - 4:30 PM

🌺 Reynolda Gardens & Village

▼

Beautiful gardens with wide paths perfect for exploring, plus charming village shops.

📍 100 Reynolda Village, Winston-Salem, NC 27106
🎯 Highlights: Formal gardens, greenhouse, wide walking paths, koi pond
🦆 Kids love looking for turtles and fish in the ponds. Plenty of space to run!
💡 Parent Tip:
Free admission! Great for wearing out the kids before the drive home. Visit the Reynolda Village shops for unique souvenirs.
5:00 PM - 6:00 PM

🍦 Farewell Ice Cream

▼

End your Winston-Salem adventure with a sweet treat!

🍨 Try Yum Yum Better Ice Cream or Ben & Jerry's at Reynolda Village
💡 Parent Tip:
Perfect way to celebrate three amazing days of family fun!

Helpful Tips for Your Trip:

🌤️ Check weather forecasts and dress in layers | 💧 Bring refillable water bottles | 📱 Download activity apps ahead of time | 🎒 Pack snacks for hangry moments | ⏰ Build in buffer time between activities for bathroom breaks and meltdowns

Have an amazing family adventure in Winston-Salem! 🎉
